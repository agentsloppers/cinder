# CINDER roadmap
## Demo 1.0 targets
- 1 large region (The Ashen Reach), 3 sub-areas (Cinder Fields, Hollow Catacombs, Spire Cliffs)
- 12 enemy types, 3 bosses (multi-phase), 30 weapons/items, 20 summonables across 2 banners
- Levelling + stats, bonfire checkpoints, souls drop/recovery, 1-2h play
## Milestones
- M1 vertical slice: run/attack/roll/lock-on, 1 enemy, summon screen  <- IN PROGRESS (built, verifying)
- M2 combat depth: parry/block, heavy attacks, 4 enemy types, bonfires, death/recovery
- M3 boss 1 (Ember Warden) + area 1 complete
- M4 gacha: 2 banners, pity, duplicates, companions
- M5 areas 2-3, bosses 2-3, 30 weapons
- M6 polish, audio, credits, perf

## Status 2026-10-20 (CINDER v0.3)
